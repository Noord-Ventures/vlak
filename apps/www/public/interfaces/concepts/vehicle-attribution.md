# Vehicle model

The 3D Workspace and EV Controls render a locally hosted adaptation of **2022 Land Rover Range Rover Evoque** by **tonielpro520**.

- Model: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/2022-land-rover-range-rover-evoque-034600db0cc94d64a7f3ccb19c7799fa
- Creator: https://sketchfab.com/tonielpro520
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Public metadata: https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/034600db0cc94d64a7f3ccb19c7799fa
- Official GLB downloaded through Sketchfab on 7 September 2026
- Shared asset: `evoque-monochrome.glb`, 4,819,464 bytes
- Local geometry: 204,453 triangles, 147,374 position/normal vertices, 66 meshes, 21 source materials
- Source listing: 204,461 triangles, 114,716 vertices; the converted GLB has the counts above

Vlak removes texture and UV data and welds identical position/normal vertices without simplifying the geometry. The source attribution is retained in the GLB metadata. Local Three.js materials match the canvas background, with fine feature edges and a narrow reverse hull for curved silhouettes. The workspace offers two line intensities and a mesh view. The EV adds actual headlight illumination on the ground. The EV scene separates the original wheel assemblies for rotation and adds an independently authored illustrative battery assembly and road map. These additions are not engineering representations of the source vehicle.
