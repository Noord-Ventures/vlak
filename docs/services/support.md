## Offboarding checklist
- Export source, configuration, fixtures, decision log, and runbook.
- Transfer or revoke credentials and confirm access ownership.
- Confirm stable operation or execute rollback.
- Record unresolved risks, incidents, and maintenance needs.
- Confirm where partner data, logs, and backups remain.
- Record final owner and closeout date.
